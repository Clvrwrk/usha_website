The Campaign

Hydrogen: the common bond.

A campaign to build the foundation of the U.S. hydrogen economy. Hydrogen already feeds our fields, fuels our refineries, and firms our power — the most abundant molecule on the planet, at work every day.

The Mission

Empower states to lead.

The Department of Energy's cancellation of hydrogen hubs erased $2.2 billion in commitments. The lesson is plain: the next era of hydrogen growth will not depend on federal megaprojects vulnerable to political cycles.

If Washington won't build it, the states will.

The State Playbook

●  Lifecycle-based, technology-neutral tax incentives
●  Revolving loan and bond financing programs
●  Plug-and-play policy templates for state legislatures

Start with the Basics

The Hydrogen Basics

Three terms, plainly.

Clean Hydrogen

Hydrogen produced through a process with a lifecycle greenhouse gas emissions rate of not greater than 4 kilograms of CO₂e per kilogram.

Electrolyzer

A hydrogen production method that separates water into hydrogen and oxygen atoms using electricity.

Fuel Cell

A system — virtually identical to a battery — that electrochemically converts hydrogen and oxygen to electricity, heat, and water.

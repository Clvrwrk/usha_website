The Hydrogen Built initiative

A national call to build the hydrogen economy.

Hydrogen Built is USHA’s initiative to elevate the real impact of hydrogen across America, and to empower the people, companies, and communities building it.

Together, we’re proving that hydrogen is more than a promise. It’s already being built.

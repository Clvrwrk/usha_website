02

Typography.

Three faces, three jobs. Space Grotesk is the voice raised (display, never below 20px, never paragraphs). Syne is the voice precise (caps, tracked, labels and buttons). Manrope is the voice at the table (body, 16px minimum). The earlier Unbounded drafts are retired.

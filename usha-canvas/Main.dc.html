About us

Technology makes hydrogen possible.
USHA builds the policy and markets to put it to work.

The United States Hydrogen Alliance is a national 501(c)(6) business association working across all 50 states to shape policy, develop markets, and connect the stakeholders needed to build durable hydrogen economies.

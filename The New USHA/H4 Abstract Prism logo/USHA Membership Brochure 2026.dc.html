WHY NOW · WHY US
Hydrogen policy is being written this year. With you or without you.
Tax credits, pipeline codes, fueling standards, hub dollars — the rules that decide whether your project pencils are moving through statehouses and federal dockets right now. Most of your industry isn't in the room.
USHA is the only hydrogen business trade association working state-by-state. Not a newsletter. Not a conference badge. An active legislative impact machine — a member said that, not us (page 3).
We advocate in Washington and every capitol, wire producers to offtakers and ports to pipelines, and move projects from pilot to price parity. Clean, domestic, union-built.

data-usha-ripple
Gold/navy dot expands from the click point. Primary + secondary buttons, 550 ms ease-out. The click IS a molecule.
data-usha-magnet
Hero CTAs lean ≤8 px toward the cursor and spring back (0.35 s overshoot). One magnetic element per view.
data-usha-glow
220 px cyan spotlight follows the cursor inside hero callouts. Never on text blocks or forms.

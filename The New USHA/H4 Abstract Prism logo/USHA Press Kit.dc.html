The United States Hydrogen Alliance (USHA) is the national coalition making America the world's hydrogen superpower. As the only hydrogen business trade association active in all 50 states, USHA advocates for hydrogen-ready policy in Washington, D.C. and every statehouse; connects producers, offtakers, ports, pipelines, universities, and unions into working partnerships; and accelerates projects from pilot to price parity. Members range from global manufacturers like Honda and Nikkiso to research institutions including the University of Delaware, Maryland, and Wyoming, alongside founding members Air Water, Taylor-Wharton, and TOMCO2 Systems. USHA also partners with allied hydrogen associations in Canada and Mexico. Its work is guided by one conviction: clean, domestic, union-built hydrogen is the element of American power.

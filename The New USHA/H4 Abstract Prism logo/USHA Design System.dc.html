01 Brand Foundation

Who we are. The United States Hydrogen Alliance (USHA) is the national coalition of producers, pipeline builders, port authorities, automakers, researchers, and unions making the United States the world's hydrogen superpower — clean, domestic, and union-built.

The idea behind the identity. Hydrogen is the smallest unit of energy in the universe; a community is the smallest unit of a nation. Our identity renders America as a field of individual dots — each one a town, a plant, a port, a person — that only becomes the map when they act together. The dot is our atom. The map is our promise.

POSITIONING
The one voice for American hydrogen — from statehouse to seaport.
PERSONALITY
Confident, kinetic, optimistic, precise. A coalition that builds.

02 Voice & Tone

USHA speaks the way it builds: short sentences, concrete nouns, momentum. Four rules govern every headline, tweet, speech, and spec sheet.

1 · Declarative
Verdicts, not hedges. "Hydrogen is the element of American power." Never "Hydrogen could potentially play a role."
2 · American-optimistic
Sea to shining sea, never us-versus-them. We celebrate places and workers by name. Patriotism is warmth, not volume.
3 · Technically fearless
We say electrolyzer, offtaker, and price parity — then earn it with a plain-English payoff in the same breath. "Codes written for molecules, not against them."
4 · Wit in the details
The joke is structural, never jokey: the gold period, "Bring your state. Bring your molecule.", H₂ as a bullet point. One wink per piece, maximum.
SAYNOT
Clean, domestic, union-builtGreen, sustainable, eco-friendly
Hydrogen jobs on the wayPotential employment opportunities
Pilot to price parityScaling our learnings
The Alliance is open.We welcome prospective stakeholder engagement
Molecules, not memos.Synergistic cross-sector solutions

Tone dial. Policy testimony: declarative + technical, wit off. Social & video: declarative + wit up, exclamation points still forbidden. Press releases: all four rules at even volume. The gold period (.) replaces the exclamation point everywhere.

03 The Logo System

One family, four instruments. Every mark is built from the same traced dot geometry — never redraw, rescale dots individually, or approximate.

A · THE MAP — PRIMARY MARK
Hero surfaces: homepage, covers, booth backwalls, video. Minimum width 120 px / 30 mm. Below that, switch to the monogram.
B · THE H — MONOGRAM
Favicons, app icons, avatars, embroidery, lapel pins. Legible to 16 px. Also the H in the "Ion Type" wordmark (USHA).
C · THE SEAL
Certification, documents, coins, embossers, social avatars. Name always centered at 12 o'clock; H₂ upright at 6. Minimum 64 px / 16 mm; below 24 px use the inner H only.

D · THE WORDMARK + LOCKUP
Unbounded 800, tracking −2%. Sub-line in Manrope 600, tracking +32%, gold on dark / royal purple on light. Sits 3–5 px under the map's purple dot row in the primary lockup.
CLEAR SPACE & THE DOT MODULE (X)

X = the diameter of the largest dot in the mark. Keep 2X clear space on all sides of every mark; 3X between the mark and any other logo. Never optically shrink clear space to fit a layout — shrink the mark.

NEVER (THE TEN COMMANDMENTS OF DON'T)
✕ Recolor dots outside the approved palettes
✕ Stretch, skew, rotate, or outline any mark
✕ Rearrange, add, or delete dots
✕ Add drop shadows or bevels (the 2e glow is the only permitted effect, dark surfaces only)
✕ Place marks on photography without a scrim
✕ Set the wordmark in any face but Unbounded
✕ Use cyan text or marks on light backgrounds
✕ Break the seal's ring text off center
✕ Use the old multi-hue seed palette
✕ Animate any mark except by Erupt / Assemble / Pulse / Settle (§08)

05 Typography

Three faces, three jobs, no exceptions. All are open-source (Google Fonts) — free for web, apps, print, and broadcast.

Unbounded — the voice raised.
Display only: headlines, wordmark, numbers that brag. Weights 800–900 (500 for marquee strips). Tracking −1% to −2%. Never below 20 px, never for paragraphs.
SPACE GROTESK — THE VOICE PRECISE
Structure: eyebrows, labels, buttons, nav, captions, seal text, tables. Weights 600–700, UPPERCASE with +8% to +34% tracking. The wider the tracking, the shorter the phrase.
Manrope — the voice at the table. Body copy, UI text, long-form, footnotes; weights 500–800; line-height 1.6–1.7.

Scale is Fibonacci (13 · 21 · 34 · 55 · 89), each step ≈ φ (1.618×) — see §06. Minimums: 16 px body on web, 11 pt body in print, 24 px on 1920×1080 slides. The gold period ends display headlines; commas and semicolons stay ice/navy.

06 Golden Ratio, Grid & Spacing

φ = 1.618 is the system's metronome. Type sizes, spacing steps, and layout splits all walk the Fibonacci sequence, so every composition shares one rhythm without anyone measuring.

SPACING TOKENS (PX)
sp-1 8 · sp-2 13 · sp-3 21 · sp-4 34 · sp-5 55 · sp-6 89 · sp-7 144
sp-1/2 inside components · sp-3/4 between elements · sp-5/6 between blocks · sp-7 between page sections
THE GOLDEN SPLIT
CONTENT · 61.8%
COUNTERWEIGHT · 38.2%

Two-column layouts split 62/38, not 50/50: hero copy vs. map, mission text vs. seal, article vs. sidebar. Full-bleed marks may break the grid; text never does.

GRIDS
Web desktop: 12 columns, 1216 px max content, 24 px gutters, 56 px margins. Web mobile (390): 4 columns, 24 px margins, stack everything, 44 px minimum hit targets. Print letter/A4: 8-column, 0.75–0.8 in margins. Slides 1920×1080: 12 columns, 96 px margins, nothing under 24 px type. The hero headline's cap-height : map height ≈ 1 : 1.618 on the homepage — hold that proportion in new heroes.

07 The Dot: Graphic Language

The dot field is the brand's texture system — America as halftone. Use the shipped geometry (§12) or generate new fields by these rules; never freehand.

Duotone. Navy dots, gold on the seed's yellow positions. For print luxe, certificates, foil.
Knockout. Ice + gold on navy. Tees, banners, one-plate reverse printing.
Rules of the field. Dots grow west → east narratively (big civic dots left, fine network right) — keep that reading. Crop generously: a map bleeding off an edge is confident; a shrunken complete map is wallpaper. One dot may be pulled out and enlarged as a graphic period or bullet. Dot fields sit behind or beside type — never under body copy without a scrim (§03). Data visualizations inherit the dot: scatter plots, unit charts, and progress indicators use circles, spectrum-colored west → east or low → high.

08 Motion

Motion is the molecule's behavior. Four verbs, in canonical order — the logo intro (16:9 and 9:16 masters) is the reference implementation.

VERBBEHAVIORTIMING
EruptEnergy leaves a single point — a gold spark, rings, then particles600–900 ms, back-out
AssembleDots fly to their places, staggered west → east1.8–2.4 s total, per-dot back-out
PulseA wave of scale (+35–45%) travels the field once1.5–2 s, gaussian falloff
SettleEverything ends at rest — no loop ends mid-motionfinal 15% of any sequence

UI micro-motion: 150–250 ms, ease-out; card hovers lift 8 px; marquees are the only permitted linear motion. Seals may rotate their ring (30–36 s/rev) and pulse their H — ring text never rotates. Honor prefers-reduced-motion: fields render assembled, seals static.

10 Print, Swag & Trade Show

Ink strategy. Full 4-color process where budget allows; the system is engineered to degrade gracefully: 2-spot (PMS 532 + 7549) for letterhead and certificates; 1-spot (PMS 532, or reversed ice-from-navy) for newsprint, forms, and embossing. Gold foil substitutes PMS 7549 on premium pieces.

Stationery. Letterhead: dotted-H top-left, address line Space Grotesk 8 pt caps +20%, gold rule footer. Business cards: navy face with knockout map full-bleed; ice reverse with name (Unbounded 800), title (Space Grotesk caps), seal at 0.5 in.

Certificates & documents. Gold Standard treatment (§07 duotone) + seal, Marcellus-free: our three faces only.

11 Advertising

The formula. One declarative headline (Unbounded, gold period) + one dot field + one gold CTA pill. Nothing else. If a stat must appear, it is the headline.

Digital sizes. 300×250 & 320×50: dotted-H + one line + CTA. 728×90 & 970×250: horizontal lockup left, headline center, CTA right. 300×600 & 9:16 stories: vertical lockup, map crop at base. 1080×1080: seal or map center, headline above. 1200×628 (link cards): homepage hero recipe at 62/38.

Video. Open with the Badge (seal) or Erupt→Assemble intro; end on the Outro end-card (QR + next videos). Lower thirds: navy bar, ice name in Space Grotesk caps, gold rule. Captions always on, Manrope 600.

Out-of-home. ≤ 7 words, map bleeding off one edge, logo + QR bottom-right at 2X clear space. Night placements use dark spectrum; transit dailies use 1-color navy.

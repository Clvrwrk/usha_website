WHAT WE DO
Advocate. Expert testimony, regulatory comments, and model legislation in every capitol — hydrogen codes written for molecules, not against them.
Connect. Tailored introductions among producers, offtakers, ports, pipelines, universities, and unions.
Accelerate. Weekly federal + state policy intelligence and monthly member strategy sessions, from pilot to price parity.
WHO WE ARE
Members span industrial gas (Air Water), mobility (Honda), cryogenics (Nikkiso, Taylor-Wharton), gas systems (TOMCO2), modular production (BayoTech), materials (Entegris), utilities (Southwest Gas), research (SwRI), and universities (Delaware, Maryland, Wyoming) — plus allied associations in Canada and México.

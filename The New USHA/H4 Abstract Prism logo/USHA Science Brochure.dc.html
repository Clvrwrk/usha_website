THE JOURNEY
Five steps from a water molecule to a national grid.
01 Produce
Electrolysis, pyrolysis, reformation with capture — every pathway that pencils, measured by carbon intensity, not color wars.
02 Store
Cryogenic liquid, compressed gas, carriers like ammonia — the physics of density is the economics of everything downstream.
03 Move
Pipelines, ports, and trailers — codes and standards decide throughput as much as steel does.
04 Use
Fuel cells, turbines, steel, ammonia, aviation — offtake is where cost curves meet demand curves.
05 Legislate
The loop closes in committee rooms: incentives, siting, safety codes. This is the step most journeys die on — and the one we own.
The thesis: steps 01–04 are engineering problems with known error bars. Step 05 is a coordination problem — and coordination problems are solved by coalitions, not by better data alone. That's the Alliance's job: turning your error bars into statute.
